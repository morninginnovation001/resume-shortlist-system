Fairness, privacy & limitations

Documented constraints for this prototype and the synthetic sample dataset.

Not approved for live hiringThis is an academic prototype. It has not been validated for employment decisions and must not be used as the sole selection criterion.
◈

Demographic fairness

The supplied feature dataset has no gender, age, name, disability, ethnicity or other protected-group columns. Therefore, group-wise selection rates, false-positive/negative rates and statistical parity cannot be computed from these CSVs.

Do not infer or synthesize protected attributes to make this audit appear complete. A lawful, consented and appropriately governed audit dataset and qualified review would be required for an empirical group analysis.

Audit incomplete by design
⌁

Data minimization

The uploaded PDF/TXT is parsed in memory. The application does not store the full resume text or original file. The review database stores only a filename label, role, matching signals, detected skill terms, and reviewer notes.

Run only in an approved environment. Filenames and reviewer notes can still contain personal information; avoid real applicant data in this demo.

Raw resume not persisted
⚖

Scoring limitations

Literal skill matching may miss synonyms and transferable skills. TF-IDF similarity measures word overlap, not meaning or competence. PDF extraction quality depends on whether the PDF contains machine-readable text.

Weights and threshold are illustrative parameters that require validation, calibration, accessibility checks, and legal review before any real-world application.

Prototype heuristic
▤

Synthetic training data

The included 5,000-row CSV is synthetic and combines 500 provided sample records with 4,500 generated feature vectors. Labels and predicted scores are not real hiring outcomes.

Performance metrics from train_baseline.py describe a random held-out split of this synthetic dataset only; they do not establish real-world accuracy or fairness.

Synthetic-only evaluation

Minimum checklist before any real pilot

  1. Obtain documented authorization and conduct privacy/security review.
  2. Validate criterion relevance with job experts; test on representative, lawful, consented data.
  3. Audit selection and error rates across legally appropriate demographic groups with qualified oversight.
  4. Provide a meaningful human review and appeal process; never auto-reject solely by score.
  5. Document model/version, thresholds, limitations, retention policy, and incident handling.

SCORING EXPLANATION

How the demo score is built

Required-skill coverage65%

Share of the detected required skills found as text matches.

TF-IDF similarity35%

Lexical overlap between the resume and job description using word and two-word features.

Not a hiring decision

The score is a prototype signal, not a validated assessment of ability. It may miss synonyms, context, seniority and transferable skills. Human review is required.

Privacy by design

  • Original resume file is not written to disk.
  • Full resume text is not saved to the SQLite database.
  • The record stores only filename, job role, scores, matched terms, and review notes.
  • Do not upload sensitive or real applicant data to an unapproved environment.
